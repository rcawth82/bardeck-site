BarDeck

Casino night and card games for your home bar or man cave.

Need help? Email robert@regiment.org and include your device (iPhone or iPad) and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

What's in BarDeck

Ten games: Blackjack, Video Poker, Roulette, Slots, Pull Tabs, Solitaire, Pyramid, Golf, Last Call and Happy Hour. You can set your bar's name, colors, sign, logo, card backs and game pieces so it feels like your own place.

Common questions

Is this real gambling?
No. BarDeck is for entertainment only. Chips are play money: they can't be bought, have no cash value, and can't be redeemed for anything.

I ran out of chips.
Tap Refill when your chips run low. You can set how many chips a player starts with in Settings.

How do I change my bar's look?
Tap the gear on the home screen. Settings has the same steps as first-time setup, plus Reset to Default Look, which keeps your chips and high scores.

How do I rearrange the games?
Press and hold any game on the home screen, choose Rearrange Games, drag the games where you want them, then tap Done.

Can I keep the screen from going to sleep?
Yes. Turn on Keep Screen On in Settings.

Does BarDeck collect my data?
No. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
