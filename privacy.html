Privacy Policy

BarDeck for iPhone and iPad · Effective November 7, 2026

BarDeck doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server.

What stays on your device

  • Your bar's setup: name, colors, look, game pieces, card backs and game order.
  • Pictures you choose for your sign, card backs, roulette wheel or jackpot screen. BarDeck only sees the pictures you pick in the photo picker, and it keeps a copy inside the app.
  • Chip balance and high scores, including any names typed in for high scores.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting BarDeck deletes it. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

Purchases

BarDeck is a paid download with no in-app purchases. Apple handles the payment; we don't receive your payment details. Chips in the app are play money with no cash value.

Children

BarDeck is meant for adults and isn't directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
